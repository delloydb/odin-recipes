Classic Banana Bread

Prep Time

15 minutes

Cook Time

1 hour

Total Time

1 hour 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • ½ cup butter, softened
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 2 ⅓ cups mashed overripe bananas (about 4-5 bananas)

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Lightly grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
  2. Combine flour, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl.
  3. In a separate bowl, beat brown sugar and butter until smooth. Stir in eggs and mashed bananas until well blended.
  4. Gradually stir banana mixture into flour mixture until just combined. Pour batter into the prepared loaf pan.
  5. Bake for about 60 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  6. Let bread cool in pan for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.

Baker's Tip

For extra flavor, you can add ½ cup of chopped walnuts or chocolate chips to the batter!
