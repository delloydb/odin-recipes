Summer Fruit Salad

A refreshing medley of seasonal fruits with zesty lemon-mint dressing

Prep Time

20 minutes

Chill Time

1 hour

Servings

6-8

Ingredients

  • 4 cups cubed seeded watermelon
  • 2 cups sliced fresh strawberries
  • 2 large fresh peaches, cut into cubes
  • 2 large nectarines, cut into cubes
  • 1 red Anjou pear, cut into cubes
  • 1 cup seedless grapes, halved
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • ¼ cup minced fresh mint (chocolate mint preferred)
  • ½ lemon, zested
  • 1 tablespoon honey (fireweed honey preferred)

Instructions

  1. 1
    Combine watermelon, strawberries, peaches, nectarines, pear, and grapes in a large mixing bowl.
  2. 2
    Whisk lemon juice, mint, lemon zest, and honey together in a small bowl.
  3. 3
    Drizzle the dressing over the fruit mixture and toss gently to coat.
  4. 4
    For best results, refrigerate the fruit salad for 1 hour before serving to allow flavors to meld.
  5. 5
    Serve chilled and enjoy!

Chef's Tips

Variations

Add blueberries, raspberries, or kiwi for extra color and flavor.

Presentation

Serve in a hollowed-out watermelon for a festive summer presentation.

Make Ahead

Can be prepared up to 4 hours in advance.
